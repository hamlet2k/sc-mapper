## Background-removal model: U²-Net-p (`public/models/u2netp.onnx`)

  Detection*, Xuebin Qin, Zichen Zhang, Chenyang Huang, Masood Dehghan, Osmar R. Zaiane, Martin Jagersand (2020).
  https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
- Licence: **Apache License 2.0** (the U-2-Net repository's licence; it has no NOTICE file). The full licence text is
  shipped next to the model as [`public/models/LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt`](public/models/LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt).
- File: the ONNX export distributed by [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 Daniel Gatis),
  `releases/download/v0.0.0/u2netp.onnx`, unmodified, sha256 `309c8469258dda742793dce0ebea8e6dd393174f89934733ecc8b14c76f4ddd8`.

## Star Citizen game data

The default bindings, action names, categories and labels come from Star Citizen's own game files
(`Data/Libs/Config/defaultProfile.xml`, `Data/Localization/english/global.ini`, `build_manifest.json`), as mirrored in the
community repository [x3nnnonn/StarCitizenDiff](https://github.com/x3nnnonn/StarCitizenDiff) (pinned commit `908b76a`).
`scripts/ensure-data.mjs` downloads them at build time and turns them into `src/data/defaults.json`, which is bundled
into the app. The raw files and the generated JSON are not committed to this repository.

This data is the property of Cloud Imperium Rights LLC / Cloud Imperium Games and is used here for a non-commercial
fan tool. It is not covered by this repository's MIT License. The StarCitizenDiff repository declares no licence of its own.
`npm run test:fixtures` also downloads a few public community keybinding files (listed in `scripts/fetch-fixtures.mjs`)
into `.tmp/` for local tests only; they are not committed or shipped.

## Product photos (`public/device-photos/`)

The built-in device templates show photos of the hardware. Manufacturer product photos are the property of their
respective manufacturers (Thrustmaster, VIRPIL, VKB, WinCtrl / WINWING, MOZA, Logitech, Honeycomb, MFG, Azeron) or their
photographers. They were cut out and resized for the templates and are used only to show where each control sits.
A few pictures (the MOZA AB6 grip variants, `moza-ab6-mh16-*`, `moza-ab6-carrierace-*`, `moza-ab6-viperace-front`)
were supplied by the project author rather than taken from vendor sites (see `scripts/device-photos/views.json`).
None of the photos are covered by this repository's MIT License. The same applies to the test fixture photos in
`scripts/fixtures/photos/`, which are made from one of the product photos.

The default stick, throttle and gamepad drawings are original artwork generated by this project's own scripts
(`scripts/gen-default-*.mjs`), contain no vendor artwork or logos, and are covered by the MIT License.
